Our Story

Homemade Happiness on Wheels

What started as one cart and a bag of sugar has grown into SugarWagon — a bright, friendly food truck built around one simple idea: dessert should feel like a celebration. Every batch of cotton candy is spun to order and every funnel cake is fried golden, right in front of you.

  • Made fresh, spun & fried to order
  • Locally sourced ingredients, always
  • Perfect for festivals, weddings & parties
