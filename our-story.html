Our Beginning

It Started With a Love for Sweet Moments

SugarWagon began with a simple belief: some of life's best memories start with something sweet.

Inspired by nostalgic fairground treats, colorful celebrations and the joy of bringing people together, we dreamed of creating something that felt like more than just another food truck.

From freshly spun cotton candy to warm funnel cakes finished with irresistible toppings, every SugarWagon treat is made to turn an ordinary stop into a moment worth remembering.

Our Little Promise Fresh treats. Happy hearts. Sweet memories.
