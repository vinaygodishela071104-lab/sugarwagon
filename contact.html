Before You Book

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers to help you plan your SugarWagon experience.

We recommend sending your request as early as possible, especially for weekends, festivals and larger celebrations. Availability is confirmed after we review your event details.

SugarWagon can be requested for birthdays, weddings, school events, corporate gatherings, festivals, markets and community celebrations.

Serving capacity depends on the menu, serving window and event format. Add your estimated guest count when sending your request.

You can request cotton candy, funnel cakes or both when completing the booking form.

Travel availability depends on the event location and schedule. Include your venue or address in the booking request for review.
